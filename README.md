This project is particularly important to me. As someone who has shifted careers, I wanted to assess the job market in the age of artificial intelligence. With the prevalence of news about layoffs accross all industries, I will use the data set to analyze trends in the job market. Additionally, I will identify which jobs and industries are most at risk of automation and those that may be replaced by AI.
